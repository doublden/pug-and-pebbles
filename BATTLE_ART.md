# Combat artwork / Боевая графика

Mode: built-in image generation, transparent raster sprite sheets. Source reference: existing public pug-idle-mobile-v5.webp, for style and identity. No private pet photos are included. Selected output sheets were isolated from adjacent cells, fitted to a shared scale and foot baseline, and packed into uniform 320 px cells and encoded as WebP with alpha. Five sheets, 12 poses each; native motion interpolates position, scale and weapon rotation without blending faces.
